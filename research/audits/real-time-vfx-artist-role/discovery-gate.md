## Candidate original, unexecuted acceptance artifact

One useful question: **If a warning effect looks correct alone, what must the VFX artist, gameplay owner and reviewers agree on before using it to communicate a live hazard amid other effects and quality settings?**

Proposed artifact: a finite **gameplay-signal handoff record**, not a studio template or player-study result.

- Gameplay owner identifies actual state/transition, target audience and spatial meaning; distinguish wind-up, active hazard and residual decoration. The effect does not define authoritative damage or collision just by drawing a shape.
- VFX author records intended primary cue, timing/shape hierarchy, cosmetic layers and candidate identity. Record which cue cannot be removed without changing meaning, without dictating universal color/brightness values.
- Integration owner pairs actual state transitions with displayed cue in a bounded reproducible sequence, retaining camera, background, overlap and quality configuration. Disagreement can be mapping/timing/rendering or authoring; do not invent cause from a screenshot.
- Context review asks whether key information remains present and visually distinguishable across the declared cases, including warning opening and end, not just the brightest peak frame. A reviewer's judgment is still not measured player understanding.
- Any reduction/variant records essential-versus-cosmetic changes and affected contexts. Reuse SOL-VFX-001 for separate cost evidence rather than claim better readability implies performance improvement.
- If the project needs evidence of recognition or reaction, specify an actual player task and observation method before collecting results; no success claim from an author demo, guided explanation or hypothetical test table. Existing playtest evidence article should constrain such interpretation.

Possible later hypothetical: a ground warning's border disappears during its opening while decorative particles stay visible. Pair actual hazard-state timing with visual evidence before choosing authoring adjustment, integration fix or escalation.
