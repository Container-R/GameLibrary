The main integrator directly read selected text on pages 3, 16, 31, 34–37, 48–49, 54–79, 93–95, 98, 104–105, 108, 112–113, 121–122 and 142. No complete-deck, video, game, audio or engine inspection is claimed.

Core source scope: pages 62–65 distinguish rejection on a false or missing fact from ranking eligible rules by the described criterion-count score. Pages 67–69 show history conditions and writeback. The proposed two-rule trace is original: inserting an additional true guard can increase the score among surviving candidates while later excluding that same rule when history changes. The trace does not establish a Valve content defect, actual tie-break output or a universal measure of semantic specificity.

The following source observations are deliberately not generalized into guarantees: response variants do not prove semantic non-repetition; writing a played/history fact does not prove the player heard the whole utterance; callback-time lookup does not prove interruption of currently playing sound, an atomic query-and-play operation or a complete concurrency manager. Source comments on additive content authoring do not imply unchanged selection outcomes. Historical performance comments are not imported as measured performance of this study.
