# Scope

Only the concise accepted role/source note and final review are published. Extended discovery/source-reading notes remain local to avoid repeated source paraphrase. No full interviews, screenshots, internal studio artifacts or copyrighted bulk are redistributed. The setting-confirm-cancel worksheet is original and unexecuted. Existing focus, formatting and accessibility evidence remains independent; it does not validate the proposed whole flow. Occupation matrix remains incomplete.

Historical hash/link report precedes the terminology-only patch; REVIEW.md final addendum and final-hashes.json identify the accepted published bytes.
