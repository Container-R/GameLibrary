## Frozen source-derived finite expectations (not native observations)

Original full pattern:

```text
{0, plural, zero {ZERO} one {ONE} two {TWO} few {FEW} many {MANY} other {OTHER}}
```

Original omission pattern differs by deleting exactly `few {FEW}`:

```text
{0, plural, zero {ZERO} one {ONE} two {TWO} many {MANY} other {OTHER}}
```

No offset, `=n`, nested argument, numeric substitution, custom number style, ordinal or duplicate keyword. Inputs are exactly representable finite integer-valued doubles 0,1,2,3,11,100. Locale IDs exactly `en` and `ar`, not a claim about all regional variants.

| n | en/full | ar/full | ar/omit-few | Independent reason |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | OTHER | ZERO | ZERO | English not1; Arabic exact0 |
| 1 | ONE | ONE | ONE | English integer1/default v0; Arabic exact1 |
| 2 | OTHER | TWO | TWO | English not1; Arabic exact2 |
| 3 | OTHER | FEW | OTHER | Arabic n%100=3; omission selects other |
| 11 | OTHER | MANY | MANY | Arabic n%100=11 |
| 100 | OTHER | OTHER | OTHER | Arabic n%100=0 but n not0/1/2 |

This is 3 configurations × 6 proposed format calls =18, not executed coverage. In the omission case, n3 is expected to format successfully as OTHER; it **fails the original full-pattern FEW acceptance intent**, not the ICU parser or locale-rule implementation.
